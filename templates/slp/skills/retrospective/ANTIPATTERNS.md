# Anti-patterns

The mistakes this way of working names, each with its sign and its way out, for naming an episode at a look back.
All share one root: a solution, a premise or a process exempted from question, while every layer added around it
carries a reasonable name.

## Giving work

| Anti-pattern                        | Sign                                                                                                            | Way out                                                               |
| ----------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Pre-solve                           | The brief fixes the solution, the criteria, what is exempt and the answer's form                                | A three-part brief; verification apart from discovery                 |
| The parachute                       | An earlier agent's choice becomes a later one's constraint                                                      | A choice recorded as a choice, open to question                       |
| Three channels locked on one answer | The worker may only change, read about and be judged by the chosen solution                                     | Reading and criteria at the level of the goal                         |
| A cause fixed in the brief          | "Enlarge the history to handle packet loss"                                                                     | Brief the symptom; reopen the premises                                |
| Feedback translated into a solution | "The bike is heavy" becomes "make the parachute lighter"                                                        | Keep feedback at the level of the goal                                |
| Phases of the plan's own making     | A state half old and half new that must compile, pass and be reviewed, kept because the plan had a phase for it | Plan to the final state; a phase only for a dependency the system has |
| A plan written as implementation    | The brief names the files, the helpers and the call order                                                       | Settle the contract; leave what lies inside a scope to its owner      |

## Working

| Anti-pattern                           | Sign                                                                         | Way out                                                |
| -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| The detour                             | Mechanism after mechanism to cover one contradiction nobody may reopen       | Reopen the contradiction                               |
| A bigger parachute                     | A parameter raised to make a structural fault rarer                          | Fix the lifecycle of what was merged                   |
| A premise nobody asks about            | Two wheels given, Guam wanted, a pedal boat returned                         | The right to object at the right time                  |
| An unquestioned redesign               | Overengineering inside the replacement                                       | Four questions for a redesign                          |
| Debate as performance                  | An agent finds something to oppose to prove its role                         | A right, not a duty; critique tied to evidence         |
| Obeying every brief                    | An agent that only does as told                                              | More room to question                                  |
| Many hands on one brake                | Two writers on one scope; a Lead writing in what it gave away                | One owner a scope                                      |
| A hidden chain of command              | One told to keep the parachute, another to drop it, the Lead unaware         | Intervention comes back to the shared state            |
| Role-play                              | Titled agents sharing context, write rights and the plan, nobody integrating | Roles by responsibility                                |
| A minted API                           | A test, or a fake in it, fixes an interface nobody settled                   | Settle the contract first, or ask                      |
| A temporary state taken for the design | A later agent tests and builds on an adapter that was meant to go            | Say in the brief what is temporary and what removes it |

## Accepting and reporting

| Anti-pattern                       | Sign                                                         | Way out                                                                    |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A bike tested on the stand         | Every part passes; nobody checked the bike stops on the road | The owner checks at the user's level; regression tests for the missed case |
| A noisy measurement                | Two agents on the CPU, a build during a benchmark            | Comparable conditions                                                      |
| A message taken as evidence        | "I will free the CPU" taken as the CPU being free            | Check the real state                                                       |
| The summarizer filters out dissent | The Human sees only "saved so many grams"                    | A shared record with what the Human needs                                  |
| A check made to pass               | Assertions loosened, product code bent for a test            | Only the behaviour working passes it                                       |

## Improving SLP itself

| Anti-pattern                         | Sign                                                      | Way out                       |
| ------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| New structure to keep an old promise | A role, checklist or approval added to keep SLP as it is  | Improve by taking away        |
| The wrong metric                     | Three good catches, so "double the critique"              | Measure outcome, not activity |
| Ceremony                             | A step that makes more traffic than value                 | Drop the step                 |
| A Lead holding everything            | Every responsibility ends at the Lead                     | Move it to the right owner    |
| Right but without context            | A Peer reports the right problem and nobody can act on it | Fix the report's form         |
